# Auto Goods Inc Context

## Canonical mission
Auto Goods Inc continuously searches for real digital market friction, builds the smallest useful digital good capable of solving validated problems, sells those goods sustainably, and converts verified distributable profit into both organizational capital and community wellness. This is the canonical ubiquitous-language source. Normative detail is in [the product contract](docs/PRODUCT-CONTRACT.md).

## Operating loop
`MARKET SIGNAL -> MARKET GAP -> EVIDENCE -> OPPORTUNITY -> VALIDATION -> VENTURE -> DIGITAL PRODUCT -> BUILD -> QA -> DEPLOY -> MARKET -> SELL -> REVENUE -> VERIFIED COSTS -> DISTRIBUTABLE PROFIT -> 50% AUTO GOODS INC -> 50% COMMUNITY WELLNESS -> VERIFIED IMPACT -> LEARNING -> REPEAT`

The repository is currently a specification foundation. It has not demonstrated autonomous operation, profitability, or community impact.

## Invariants
Only positive `DistributableProfit` may be distributed. Money uses integer minor currency units. Auto Goods Inc and community wellness receive equal shares, subject only to deterministic one-minor-unit remainder handling. Community wellness is restricted to free food, free housing, free healthcare, free education, and free community space. The v0.1 community half is divided equally among the five domains. Ordinary agents, ventures, product code, marketing systems, and runtime configuration cannot modify the split. Failure freezes treasury.

## Evidence language
Every material analytical claim is exactly `OBSERVED`, `INFERRED`, `SPECULATIVE`, or `NOT_COMPUTABLE`. Observations require provenance. Inferences identify observed inputs. Speculation cannot independently authorize spend or promotion. Missing inputs remain unresolved. Never invent buyers, prices, demand, TAM, CAC, unit economics, or impact.

## Canonical nouns
Use without synonym drift: `MarketSignal`, `MarketGap`, `Opportunity`, `ValidationExperiment`, `VentureCandidate`, `Venture`, `Product`, `Customer`, `Transaction`, `LedgerEvent`, `DistributionPeriod`, `ProfitDistribution`, `CommunityOrganization`, `CommunityProgram`, `CommunityAllocation`, `ImpactReceipt`, `Evidence`, `PolicyDecision`, `AuthorityGrant`, and `RunReceipt`.

A `MarketSignal` is one observed indication of friction or demand. A `MarketGap` is a repeated unmet or poorly served state transition supported by multiple signals. An `Opportunity` is an evidence-backed commercial thesis. A `VentureCandidate` is under resource evaluation. A `Venture` is a promoted commercial entity. A `Product` is a concrete digital good owned by a venture.

## Boundaries
Commercial scoring optimizes pain, buyer reality, distribution, automation, economics, proof cost, and evidence quality. Community allocation separately optimizes need, service gap, funding utility, recipient capacity, verification, and output. Community need never affects venture scoring. Hypothesis creation and grading are separate authorities. Allocation and verification are separate. Ventures do not control treasury. Accounting and routing are deterministic software responsibilities.

Auto Goods Inc is independent from Neon Genie. Its patterns may inspire opportunity mining, evidence labeling, commercial modeling, blocked-transition analysis, validation, and agent decomposition, but no hard runtime dependency is permitted without a concrete reviewed need.
