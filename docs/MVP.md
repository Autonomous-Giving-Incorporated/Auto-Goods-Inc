# MVP Acceptance Contract

## Canonical milestone
`AUTO_GOODS_LOOP_001` is the single canonical identifier for the first system-level proof of the Auto Goods Inc thesis.

It passes only when one traceable real-world run demonstrates:
1. real provenance-backed `MarketSignal`;
2. evidence- and buyer-gated `Opportunity`;
3. bounded `ValidationExperiment` with external validation and independent judgment;
4. policy promotion from `VentureCandidate` to `Venture`;
5. usable `Product` built and passing QA;
6. approved production deployment;
7. real `Customer` acquired without fabricated attribution;
8. real settled payment mapped to immutable `LedgerEvent` records;
9. reconciled `DistributionPeriod` with positive `DistributableProfit`;
10. deterministic integer-money 50/50 invariant passing;
11. valid `CommunityAllocation` for verified organization and eligible program;
12. independently verified `ImpactReceipt` recording actual output or honest unresolved state.

All steps share traceable identifiers and evidence. Simulated, test, refunded, disputed, or unsettled payments do not qualify. Projections do not satisfy profit. Allocation without delivery evidence does not satisfy verified impact. Private customer data never appears publicly.

Any missing requirement, failed invariant, unauthorized action, or unresolved reconciliation blocks the milestone. The result is failed or `NOT_COMPUTABLE`, never partial success.

The evidence packet includes policy/schema versions, sources, transitions, grants, run receipts, release evidence, payment/reconciliation references, split calculation, allocation approval, impact verification, privacy review, exceptions, and incidents. One completed loop does not establish general autonomy, repeatable profitability, predictive scoring validity, or durable impact.
