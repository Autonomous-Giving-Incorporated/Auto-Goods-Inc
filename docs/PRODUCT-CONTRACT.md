# Product Contract

## Mission
Auto Goods Inc is intended to find real digital market friction, validate commercial opportunities, build and sell the smallest useful digital goods, and turn verified positive profit into organizational capital and community wellness. The repository is currently a foundation and does not claim the loop has completed.

## Operating contract
`MarketSignal -> MarketGap -> Evidence -> Opportunity -> ValidationExperiment -> VentureCandidate -> Venture -> Product -> Customer -> Transaction -> LedgerEvent -> DistributionPeriod -> ProfitDistribution -> CommunityAllocation -> ImpactReceipt -> learning`

The canonical mission and business loop are in [CONTEXT.md](../CONTEXT.md).

## Separate functions
The venture function optimizes demonstrated pain, buyer identity and access, spending and willingness-to-pay evidence, distribution, automation, proof cost, complexity, economics, recovery time, support burden, and evidence quality. The community function separately optimizes demonstrated need, service gap, marginal funding utility, recipient capacity, verification quality, and actual output. Community need never increases commercial score.

## Evidence and promotion
Material claims use `OBSERVED`, `INFERRED`, `SPECULATIVE`, or `NOT_COMPUTABLE`. Never fabricate buyers, demand, price, TAM, CAC, economics, or impact. Promotion to `Venture` requires external validation such as a paid pilot, preorder, actual usage, waitlist conversion, successful paid test, signed LOI, or direct customer evidence. An LLM assertion is insufficient.

## Economic contract
Only positive `DistributableProfit` may be split. Auto Goods Inc and community wellness receive equal shares within deterministic one-minor-unit remainder handling. Eligible community wellness is limited to free food, housing, healthcare, education, and community space. Ordinary agents and ventures cannot change the invariant.

## Constraints
Initial limits are five active `ValidationExperiment` records and two simultaneous builds. Prefer low proof cost, fast revenue paths, high automation, low regulatory complexity, and low support. Initially avoid medical decision software, individualized financial advice, physical inventory, heavily regulated marketplaces, custom enterprise consulting, and support-heavy products.

Auto Goods Inc must operate independently from Neon Genie. Patterns may be adapted without a hard runtime dependency.
