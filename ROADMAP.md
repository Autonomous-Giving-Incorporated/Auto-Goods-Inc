# Roadmap

Progress is gated by demonstrated acceptance evidence, not dates or forecasts.

## Phase 0: Constitution, current
Define language, schemas, deterministic policies, authority, lifecycle, security, and verification fixtures. Exit requires internally consistent docs plus valid schemas and policies.

## Phase 1: Market Oracle
Convert public research into provenance-backed `MarketSignal`, `MarketGap`, `Opportunity`, and ranked `VentureCandidate` records. Humans choose what proceeds.

## Phase 2: Validation Engine
Run bounded `ValidationExperiment` records with independent judging. Outputs are `PROMOTE`, `HOLD`, or `KILL`.

## Phase 3: Goods Foundry
For an approved `Venture`, produce product specification, repository, plan, code, tests, staging, landing page, analytics, and Stripe product. Initial production launch is human-confirmed.

## Phase 4: Growth
Enable bounded SEO, content, pricing, channel, conversion, and email experiments with claims controls, spend ceilings, attribution, and rollback.

## Phase 5: Closed Economic Loop
Reconcile a real settled customer payment, calculate positive `DistributableProfit`, and verify deterministic 50/50 allocation.

## Phase 6: Community Loop
Operate verified organization and program registries, approved allocation and payment records, independent impact verification, and a privacy-preserving transparency surface.

[`AUTO_GOODS_LOOP_001`](docs/MVP.md) is the first proof of the full thesis. Until it completes with real evidence, do not claim demonstrated autonomy, profitability, or impact. Scoring calibration, evidence-driven community weighting, higher autonomy, and additional payment rails are deferred until operating evidence and governance justify them.
